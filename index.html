01 · Featured · made-up data

EcoFlow - Industrial Wastewater Treatment Analytics

A monitoring dashboard for an imaginary wastewater treatment plant. The data is made up (synthetic) and created by a Python script, but the engineering behind it is real: the dashboard tracks how much pollution the plant removes from the water, and how much electricity and chemicals it uses to do it.

  • How well the plant removes COD, BOD and suspended solids, the standard measures of how dirty water is
  • Electricity and chemical use for every cubic metre of water treated
  • Days when the process goes wrong, and sensor readings that cannot be trusted
  • A what-if comparison: normal running against improved pump and dosing settings

All the data is clearly labelled as made up. The notes in the repository explain every calculation in plain language, so I can stand behind each number.

Research · 2022 to 2023

Reusing spent activated carbon

Activated carbon is a cleaning material that eventually gets used up. I studied carbon that had been used to purify glycerine: could it be regenerated with NaOH and used again? I compared its properties before and after, and identified the best NaOH concentration.

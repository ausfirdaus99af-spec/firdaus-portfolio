# EcoFlow - Methodology

EcoFlow is my portfolio project: an analytics pipeline for an imaginary industrial wastewater treatment plant. Every number in this project is made up (synthetic), generated by `scripts/generate_data.py`. No real plant, client or employer data is used, referenced or represented.

## Why made-up data

I cannot publish operating data from a real facility, and public datasets rarely include the failures that make monitoring interesting. Making the data up instead gives three advantages:

1. Full control of the ground truth, so every calculation can be verified against known inputs.
2. Reproducibility: the generator is deterministic for a given seed (default 42), so anyone can regenerate the exact dataset and audit the results.
3. Deliberate imperfection: process upsets, maintenance windows and sensor artefacts are injected on purpose, so the cleaning and KPI logic can be demonstrated honestly.

## What is generated

Hourly records over 120 days, 4 July 2026 to 31 October 2026 inclusive (2,880 hours per scenario, 5,760 rows in total), for two scenarios that share the same influent and the same event times:

- **baseline**: conventional operation.
- **optimised**: modelled VFD aeration control (about 12 percent lower electricity per m3) and improved coagulant dose control (about 15 percent lower dose, with about 1 percentage point better TSS removal).

### Influent patterns

- Flow: mean about 200 m3/h with a diurnal pattern (higher during the day), a weekly pattern (lower at weekends) and gaussian noise, clipped to a plausible 120 to 300 m3/h band. Maintenance windows reduce flow by about 40 percent.
- pH: around 7.2, bounded to 6.2 to 8.8.
- COD: 450 to 900 mg/L, correlated with flow surges and dips.
- BOD: 0.45 to 0.55 times COD, so BOD never exceeds COD.
- TSS: 150 to 450 mg/L, loosely correlated with flow.

### Treatment performance

Under normal operation, effluent concentrations are derived from the influent and a sampled removal efficiency with noise: COD removal 85 to 92 percent, BOD removal 90 to 96 percent, TSS removal 90 to 97 percent. Effluent pH is held in a tighter 6.8 to 7.6 band to reflect neutralisation. Effluent BOD is constrained to never exceed effluent COD.

### Process events

- **Upsets**: 4 to 6 multi-hour events per run, at seed-derived times that are identical in both scenarios so the scenarios stay comparable. During an upset, removal efficiencies degrade sharply (for example COD removal drops to 55 to 70 percent) and effluent COD and TSS spike. Rows are tagged `treatment_status = "upset"`.
- **Maintenance**: two planned 8 hour windows with flow reduced by about 40 percent, tagged `treatment_status = "maintenance"`.

### Sensor artefacts (anomalies)

Roughly 0.5 to 0.8 percent of rows per scenario carry an injected sensor artefact, tagged in `anomaly_type`:

- `sensor_spike`: a single absurd reading, for example pH 14 or COD multiplied by 5.
- `sensor_stuck`: 3 to 6 consecutive identical readings in one column.
- `missing`: a missing value in one sensor column.
- `out_of_range`: a zero or negative value where that is physically impossible.

Artefacts are injected after the physical values are computed, so they represent bad readings rather than real process changes, and they are placed only on normal-status rows so process events and data-quality issues remain separable. The cleaning step in `scripts/analytics.py` detects them both by tag and by independent plausibility range checks, and removes them before any KPI is aggregated.

## Pipeline

`scripts/build_dashboard_data.py` runs the generator, writes `data/ecoflow_hourly.csv`, cleans the data, aggregates it to daily and whole-period KPIs, and writes `data/dashboard_data.json` in a fixed structure consumed by the dashboard. The JSON is strict: missing numbers are written as null, never NaN or Infinity.

## What this project does NOT represent

- It is not data from any real plant, and no resemblance to any actual facility is intended.
- It makes no regulatory or compliance claims; discharge limits are not modelled.
- The parameter values are plausible for teaching and demonstration, not calibrated to any specific treatment technology.
- Nothing in this project is operational, design or dosing guidance.
